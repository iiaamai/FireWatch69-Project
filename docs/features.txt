FIREWATCH 69 — FEATURE INVENTORY
=================================

A. Barangay Officials Web Application
- Login and account/role management
- Dashboard: active incidents, response metrics, responders, map
- Incident & Blotter Management: create, review, assess, assign, update, resolve, and document incidents
- Responder tracking and assignment monitoring
- Team management
- Evacuation center management
- Fire hydrant management: location, status, verification, inspection history
- Incident history and search/filter
- User management
- In-app notifications and real-time alerts
- Activity logs
- Analytics and reports
- Incident heatmap and high-risk-area analytics
- Map/location monitoring with selectable layers
- Account/profile

B. Emergency Responder Mobile App
- Login and profile
- Assigned incidents and incident details
- Navigation/GPS and map
- Nearby fire hydrant pins and status
- Team coordination
- Push/in-app notifications
- Response status updates
- Reinforcement requests

C. Registered Resident Mobile App
- Login/account
- Home and nearby alerts
- Incident/SOS reporting with location
- Map showing permitted incidents, evacuation centers, and public hydrants
- Permitted team/response progress tracking
- Report details and own report history
- Alerts and profile

D. Guest Resident Mobile App
- Guest access/verification
- Incident/SOS reporting with location and abuse controls
- Public map and permitted hydrant/evacuation information
- Permitted response updates
- Alerts and report details where guest session permits

E. Shared services
- Authentication and authorization
- Central incident and blotter data
- Map/geolocation service
- Responder location service
- Event/history service
- Notification service
- Analytics service

Scope advice:
- Implement a minimum viable end-to-end reporting and dispatch workflow first.
- Confirm live video, guest verification, public responder tracking, and advanced risk analytics before committing to them as mandatory features.
