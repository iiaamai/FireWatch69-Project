FIREWATCH 69 — DOCUMENT / ACCESS CONTROL NOTES
==============================================

Purpose:
Define access boundaries for incident records, media, responder locations, and administration.

Suggested role definitions (confirm with stakeholders):
- system_admin: technical configuration and account/role administration
- barangay_official: incident/blotter oversight, records, reports, and permitted management tasks
- coordinator: authorized incident assessment, dispatch, and escalation
- responder: assigned incident details, team coordination, response status, and authorized GPS features
- registered_resident: submit reports and view own report status plus public alerts
- guest_resident: submit a limited report through a validated guest session and view public information

Access principles:
- Deny by default; grant only necessary permissions.
- Check ownership and role on the backend for every protected request.
- Separate authentication (who is the user) from authorization (what may they do).
- Keep incident details, contact details, media, and live responder locations private unless specifically approved for release.
- Use short-lived signed URLs or authenticated media endpoints for private attachments.
- Never store plaintext passwords, access tokens, or API secrets in logs.
- Log sensitive administrative actions.
- Validate guest submissions, rate-limit SOS/report endpoints, and add anti-abuse controls.
- Apply retention rules to GPS history, incident media, and personal data.

Questions to resolve:
- Who may set severity and who may override it?
- Who may create or edit official blotter records?
- Who may assign/reassign responders?
- May residents see live responder positions, or only progress/ETA?
- Are hydrant pins public, responder-only, or staff-only?
- Who may export reports and view sensitive attachments?
- How are guest reports linked to follow-up without a permanent account?
- What data retention and consent notices are required?

This document is a proposal, not a finalized legal or barangay policy.
