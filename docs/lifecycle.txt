FIREWATCH 69 — INCIDENT LIFECYCLE
==================================

Proposed workflow (must be validated with barangay/BFP stakeholders):
1. Submitted: report received through app or encoded by staff from phone/walk-in.
2. Under review: authorized staff checks information and possible duplicates.
3. Verified: incident accepted as valid for response.
4. Dispatched: responder/team assignment has been created.
5. Responding / En route: assigned responder is moving toward the incident.
6. On scene: responder reports arrival.
7. Resolved: immediate response is reported complete; resolution details are recorded.
8. Closed: authorized staff completes required documentation and closes the record.
9. Rejected: authorized staff rejects a duplicate, invalid, or out-of-scope report with a reason.

Possible exceptional transitions:
- Reopen a resolved/closed incident only through an authorized workflow.
- Reassign an incident while preserving the prior assignment history.
- Request reinforcement without losing the original assignment.
- Update severity with actor, timestamp, previous value, new value, and reason.

Rules:
- Do not assume that every incident must have the same workflow or a formal blotter record.
- Define who can perform each transition and which fields are mandatory.
- Record transitions as append-only IncidentEvent entries.
- Use server timestamps as the authoritative event time; retain reported/captured time separately when useful.
- Do not derive official response-time metrics from client device clocks alone.
